From building interfaces to understanding the data behind them.

I am an M.Sc. Information Technology graduate transitioning from front-end development to data analytics. My professional experience taught me how operational data reaches a user; my portfolio work focuses on what that data can explain.

At MD Infotech, I built and maintained real-time monitoring dashboards for wind and solar energy systems. I worked with KPIs such as energy output, machine status and performance trends, validated dashboard values against live field readings during onsite testing, redesigned legacy layouts, and collaborated with backend and remote teams to resolve data discrepancies.

I then applied those habits to self-directed analytics projects: collecting and cleaning data, designing warehouses and star schemas, writing SQL, building Python ETL workflows, creating DAX measures, and presenting findings through Power BI dashboards.

Beyond technical work, I served as Department Symposium Vice President at Bishop Heber College, coordinating activities involving 180+ students, participating colleges, faculty and support teams. That experience strengthened my communication, coordination, decision-making and ability to stay organized when many tasks move at once.
